EHow Expert Widget works

The widget does nothing on its own. It shows what it saved last time, and only goes out to fetch new data when you press Refresh. Your CV is read on this PC and never leaves it.

  • You do this
  • Happens on your PC
  • Goes to the internet
  • The widget decides

1. When it starts

No internet needed.

You start Expert Widgetor your computer starts it for you at login
Is this the very first start?
Yes
Creates Documents\ExpertWidget\ with an empty CV\ folder, config.json, employers.json and watchlist.json.
No
Uses the files that are already there.
this PCShows the round E buttonWhere you left it, with a badge counting news you have not opened yet. Double-click to open the panel.
this PCShows what it saved last timeEmployers and news from the last Refresh. Nothing is fetched until you press Refresh.

2. When you press Refresh

Takes about 5 to 30 seconds.

You press Refreshin the panel, or "Refresh now" in the tray menu
this PC1Read your CVLooks in Documents\ExpertWidget\CV\ for one PDF, Word or text file.
Exactly one CV, and could it be read?
Yes
Counts the keywords from config.json in the CV and works out your domain mix, e.g. Automotive 60 %, Energy storage 40 %. Up to 4 domains. The CV text is then thrown away.
No
Keeps the domain mix from last time and tells you why ("No CV found", "more than one CV", "scanned PDF").
Is the PC online?
Yes
Carry on to step 2.
No
Stops here. Shows "Offline, showing data from …" and keeps the saved news.

2Fetch news, both at the same time

internetNews sitesingenieur.de, startbase, Munich Startup, messen.de, produktion.de and others from config.json. Every Refresh. Only sites that fit your domains.
internetGDELT news searchOne search per domain in your mix, using only keywords from config.json, and one per watchlist company, by its name. Never your CV. At most every 15 minutes. In between, its last results are reused.
Did GDELT find something for every domain?
Yes
Carry on.
No
If the Google News fallback is switched on (it is off by default), search Google News for the missing domains only.
Did every source answer properly?
Yes
Saves the new items.
No
Keeps that source's old items and shows "One source could not be loaded". After 3 failed Refreshes in a row it is marked needs attention.
this PC3Translate to EnglishNew German headlines and summaries are translated by a small model on this PC. No online service.
this PC4Sort out what to show Employers: from employers.json, only the segments for your domains, strongest domain first. Companies you already visited move to the end. Watchlist: every company in watchlist.json, whatever your domains. News that names one gets a Watchlist label and comes first. News: duplicates removed; each item gets a tag (job cuts, hiring, funded startup, career fair) and a domain. Items outside your domains, older than 7 days, or thumbed down are dropped. Thumbed-up items always stay.
this PC5Update the panel
Domain mixyour mix and "Updated today, 07:12"
Employerscareer-page links
Watchlistyour companies, news counts
News feedfilters, EN switch, source status

3. What you do with it

All remembered on this PC.

Click a companyOpens its career page. Marked visited and moves to the end.
Click a news cardOpens the article. Marked Seen and sinks to the bottom.
Thumbs downRemoves the article for good, also after Refresh.
Thumbs upKeeps the card, even after it gets old.

Where things live

Your files: you edit these

Documents\ExpertWidget\
  • CV\ exactly one CV
  • config.json keywords, news sites, settings
  • employers.json companies and career links
  • watchlist.json companies you keep an eye on

Widget memory: it keeps these itself

%APPDATA%\Expert Widget\
  • profile.json your domain mix (not the CV)
  • feed-cache.json saved news
  • interactions.json visited, seen, thumbs
  • health.json which sources work
  • ui.json button position, theme

Edit a file in Documents\ExpertWidget\, save it, and press Refresh to see the change.
